# FIZLY Zero launch: measurement readout

*Synthetic data. FIZLY is a fictional brand.*

## Bottom line

- The launch video pays back. Short video and online video together returned about 4.5 won of shelf sales per won over the half year (90% interval 3.9-5.1), most of it in stores. At an assumed 30% contribution on shelf sales, break-even is 3.3, and even the low end of the interval clears it.
- Sustain video after the flight sits near the edge: its last won returns about 3.7 (90%: 3.3-4.1) against a break-even of 3.3. Hold the weight and find the edge with a step-down test.
- Do not judge video on online numbers. The platform credits 2.2 times the online orders video adds, and video's online return alone is 0.70: about 85% of what video earns is in stores and in the weeks after a flight, where no pixel looks.
- Scale creative A. Opening on the product in the first second lifted ad recall +5.0pp more than the lifestyle opening (95% CI +2.3 to +7.8).
- Search and retail media look best in platform reports and are the weakest lines. The model puts them at 0.7 and 0.8, with intervals topping out at 1.78 and 2.20, under the online break-even of 2.22 even at the top. Cut both 30% and confirm with holdout tests.

## What we measured

| Question | Method | Answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Did the launch video build awareness among people it reached? | Brand lift survey, 20% holdout | Ad recall +11.6pp, awareness +5.8pp (+4.9pp across everyone reached) | High for recall and awareness; consideration and intent directional only |
| How many online orders did video add? | Conversion lift, 10% user holdout | 4,524 orders in six weeks; online return 0.70 per won | High (95% CI 0.54-0.85); confirmed by the geo test's online part |
| With store sales, what did video return in the test weeks? | Geo test, 12 regions dark for 6 weeks | 1.68 in the window, 2.27 with a two-week cooldown | Medium: wide interval; a short window understates a launch |
| Over the half year, what does video return? | MMM calibrated to the geo test | 4.50 for both video channels together | Medium-high on the total; low on the split between the two video channels |
| Is awareness growing in the market? | Independent tracker, monthly | From 9% at launch to 17% by week 103 | Level only: a monthly wave of 1,000 cannot say what caused a change under 5.7pp |

## What we are not sure about

- The contribution margins: 30% of shelf sales and 45% online are our assumptions; at 25-40% on shelf sales, break-even runs from 4.0 to 2.5.
- How the video return splits between short video and online video: they build the same awareness and no test so far separated them. The Q1 test moves short video alone for long enough to see carry-over.
- The model's marginal return for sustain video (3.7): in this lab the model runs high on it; the Q1 test measures it directly.
- Returns beyond week 104: the model assumes awareness keeps fading at the rate it learned.

## What we need from you

- Region-level sell-out panel every Monday (the geo test cannot run on national totals).
- Server-side purchase events, so more than 75% of online orders match: a better match rate narrows every online read.
- Tell the agency about local competitor launches and retailer promotions during tests; that is the only reliable way to drop a contaminated region.
- Agree the rules before each test (metrics, holdouts, read dates) and do not ask for early reads.

## Was measuring worth it?

Measuring cost about 81M KRW of sales in the go-dark regions and about 8M KRW of online sales in the holdout. Lab check (uses the simulated truth): halving sustain video would save 101M KRW of media and lose 300M KRW of shelf sales by week 104, a true return of 3.0 per won (the model said 3.7).
